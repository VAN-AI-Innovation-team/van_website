How the work runs

  • One in-person meeting per week per bureau; other work runs online
  • No regular in-person meetings during midterm and final exam periods
  • Official certificate of activity for members who stay one year or longer
  • Outstanding members recognised each semester; top members referred for internships and hiring

The Structure

The structure you join

A representative council and operating staff work alongside an Advisory Council of professional advisers and faculty advisers. Because the structure runs independently of student-government terms, one semester’s work becomes the next semester’s foundation.

01

Year-round operation

Academic exchange continues through the year rather than following election cycles.

02

Advisory structure

Faculty advisers and professional advisers are built into research and projects.

03

Records that accumulate

Work is documented in the archive so the next cohort can build on it.
